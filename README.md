# 🔱 HONK HUB

> Advanced Roblox utility script focused on precision, targeting and player visualization.

---

✨ Features

🎯 General Silent Aim

The General Silent Aim automatically searches for the closest valid player inside your FOV.

When a player enters the red FOV circle, the system detects the best target based on proximity and redirects your shots toward them, allowing the bullet to travel directly toward the selected target without requiring your crosshair to be perfectly aligned with the player.

Highlights:

- 🔴 FOV-based target detection
- - 🎯 Automatically finds targets inside the FOV
- 📍 Prioritizes the closest player
- ⚡ Automatic target selection
- 🧭 Precise shot redirection

---

🧠 Intelligent Silent Aim

The Intelligent Silent Aim gives you complete control over your target.

Instead of automatically selecting someone based on the FOV, you can choose a specific player, and the system will prioritize that player for your shots.

Highlights:

- 🧠 Manual target selection
- 🎯 Locks onto the chosen player
- 🔄 Target-focused shot redirection
- 👤 Easy player selection

---

🛡️ Team Check

The Team Check prevents Silent Aim from targeting players who are on the same team as you.

This helps avoid accidentally redirecting shots toward teammates and keeps the targeting system focused on valid opponents.

Highlights:

- 🛡️ Detects team affiliation
- 🚫 Ignores teammates
- 🎯 Targets opponents only
- ⚙️ Works with Silent Aim systems

---

👁️ ESP

The ESP system provides additional player visibility, making it easier to identify and track players in the game.

It helps you maintain awareness of player positions and locate targets more easily.

Highlights:

- 👁️ Player visualization
- 📍 Easier target identification
- 🎯 Improved situational awareness
- ⚡ Real-time player tracking

⚙️ How It Works

The targeting system continuously evaluates players around you and determines which targets are valid according to your configuration.

General Silent Aim:

"FOV → Detect Players → Validate Target → Find Closest → Redirect Shot"

Intelligent Silent Aim:

"Choose Player → Validate Target → Prioritize Selected Player → Redirect Shot"

Team Check:

"Player Detected → Check Team → Same Team? Ignore : Valid Target"
